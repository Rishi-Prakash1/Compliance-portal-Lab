# OT Incident Response Plan  (Doc ID: IRP-OT-004, v1.2, Approved 2024-08-01)

## 1. Scope
Applies to all OT assets of Plant Unit 2, including those notified as Protected Systems.

## 4. External Reporting
Cyber security incidents affecting OT assets shall be reported to CERT-In and the
sectoral CSIRT within 24 hours of the incident being confirmed by the OT Security Lead.

## 5. Evidence Preservation
Volatile evidence is captured before containment where safe. Logs are preserved
from the SIEM for the duration of the investigation.

## 7. Review
This plan is reviewed every two years.
